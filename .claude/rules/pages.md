# Page rules (src/pages)

- Pages consume the normalised offer object from `getOffers()`, never raw
  Airtable field names.
- Every WhatsApp link is built by `enquiryLink()` or uses `WA_LINK` /
  `WA_DISPLAY` from `src/lib/whatsapp.mjs`. No inline `wa.me` strings and no
  second copy of the phone number.
- Slugs come from `generateSlug()` + `dedupeSlug()` in `src/lib/slug.mjs`,
  iterated in the same order as the other pages, or offer pages and the sitemap
  will disagree.
- Category URL segments are `category.toLowerCase().replace(/\s+/g, '-')`
  everywhere. Do not introduce a second convention.
- JSON-LD goes through `src/lib/schema.mjs`. Do not hand-write structured data
  in a page, and do not assert a price basis the normaliser did not provide.
- `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml` and `/llms.txt` are generated by the `.ts`
  endpoints here. The copies in `public/` are stale and get overwritten; edit
  the endpoints.
- `llms.txt` lists categories by hand. Update it when a category appears in the
  base.
- Pages are single files with scoped `<style>` and inline `<script>`. No new
  framework or Astro integration without a decision-log entry.
- The category request form posts with `mode: 'no-cors'`. Its confirmation text
  must not claim the request succeeded, because the response is opaque.
- Keep `content-visibility: auto` on cards; the grid is ~4,000 cards.

After a change: `npm run build`, open `dist/index.html` and one
`dist/offers/*/index.html`, and check a case-priced and a unit-priced offer show
the right figure under the right label.
