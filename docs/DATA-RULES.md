# Data rules

Invariants on offer data, each with the bug it prevents. When one of these is
violated the site publishes a wrong price or a broken message to a trade buyer,
so they are checked before anything else.

## 1. Public-safe fields only

`FIELDS` in `src/data/airtable.mjs` is the complete list of what the site may
read. Never in it: supplier name, buy price, margin, internal notes, contact
details of counterparties. Adding a field is a deliberate act with a reason in
the commit body.

Also: rows whose name starts with `TestBrand` or `TestProduct` are dropped, and
only rows with `{Public Listing}='Yes'` are fetched.

## 2. Amount and basis come from the same string

`Price Per Unit & Case` looks like `EUR 112.44/case (6pk) · EUR 18.74/unit`.
The headline `amount` and its `priceBasis` are both taken from the first
`·`-separated part. `Price Display` is only a fallback when that field is empty,
and then `priceBasis` is `''` and no page may assert a basis.

**Bug prevented:** 49 of 173 offers showed a per-unit number under a "/ case"
label. Glenmorangie 12YO showed EUR 18.74 for a 6-bottle case instead of
EUR 112.44. Fixed 2026-08-08.

**Downstream:** `schema.mjs` derives `eligibleQuantity.unitText` from
`priceBasis` (case/pack → `case`, unit/btl/can/piece/jar → `unit`, unknown →
omit). Hard-coding `case` there mispriced 60 offers to search engines. Fixed
2026-08-14.

## 3. Sorting compares per-unit figures

`unitAmount` is the per-unit price for every offer. When only a case price
exists, divide by the pack size, found in this order: `(Npk)` in the price
string, `N x` at the start of `Public Spec`, then the `PCS/Case` field. If no
pack size exists anywhere the case figure stays as an honest fallback.

**Bug prevented:** case-only offers sorted by case price against everyone
else's unit price (48 of 173). Offers with no price sank to the bottom instead
of leading "price ascending" as if free.

## 4. Whole cases

`qty` is `Math.round(Stock Cases)`. A fractional case count means units were
entered as cases in Airtable. The site rounds; the `offer-data-validator` skill
flags the row so Airtable gets fixed.

**Bug prevented:** 10 offers showed "12,412.3 cases".

## 5. Variant lists are not names

A name like `Nivea Roll On 50ml — Bright & Dry, Silk Touch, Pearl` is split at
the dash into `name` + `variants` only when the tail has two or more commas and
the head is at least 8 characters. The `Variant` field, when present, wins.
Single-dash product names stay intact.

**Bug prevented:** one name was 365 characters and broke the card grid.

## 6. Every WhatsApp link goes through `enquiryLink()`

`src/lib/whatsapp.mjs` owns the number (`353872382368`, digits only) and the
prefilled-message builder. Each clause (spec, price, basis) is omitted when its
field is empty rather than rendered blank.

**Bug prevented:** "quote for Indomie Noodles () listed at USD ." reaching a
customer's chat. 24 of ~2,500 live offers have no spec; at least one has no
price.

## 7. Live and snapshot paths produce identical output

`normalize()` (live) and `renormalizeSnapshotOffer()` (snapshot) must apply the
same corrections. Production builds from the snapshot, so a correction that only
exists in `normalize()` is invisible on the site until the snapshot is
regenerated by the refresh Action or `npm run sync-offers`.

**Bug prevented:** a fallback deploy resurrecting the old per-unit/per-case
mix-up after it had been fixed in the live path.

## 8. The snapshot is only overwritten by a successful live fetch

`fetch-offers.mjs` exits 1 if `source !== 'live'`. The refresh Action therefore
never commits an empty or partial catalogue after a network or Airtable error.

## 9. Retry only what is worth retrying

`fetchWithRetry()` retries 429, 5xx and network errors up to 4 times with
`Retry-After` or exponential backoff from 500 ms with jitter. It does **not**
retry 401, 403 or 422: those are configuration faults and repeating them only
delays the report.

## 10. On money, refuse rather than guess

This is the n8n ingestion rule and it applies to the site too. A price range,
an average, or two different prices for the same goods is sent to review, not
resolved by picking one. A silent 10 to 40 percent error on a number that ends
up in a client quote is worse than a review line a person settles in seconds.

## Categories in the base today

Beer, Champagne, Confectionery, Grocery, Other, Other FMCG, Soft Drinks,
Spirits, Toiletries, Wine. Missing `Category` defaults to `Other`. `llms.txt`
lists a fixed subset by hand; update it when a category is added.

## Duty tiers

`tier` carries `Bond/Customs Status`: **T1** is under-bond / export, **T2** is EU
duty-paid. The guide at `/guides/t1-vs-t2-duty-status/` explains this to buyers.
Do not invent a tier when the field is empty.

## Checking offer rows before they reach Airtable

Run the `offer-data-validator` skill (`python3
.claude/skills/offer-data-validator/validate_offers.py <file>`) on any CSV or
XLSX of offers before import. It checks rules 2 to 5 above plus truncated brands,
incomplete pack specs and currency sanity, and exits non-zero on price errors.
